Writing a sales CV

Salespeople sell results. Your CV has to prove them: figures, clients, growth. A sales recruiter reads the numbers first.

1. An opening line that says where you sell

In two lines: your job, your sector, your strength. For example: "Field sales representative for 4 years in food distribution, specialised in opening new points of sale."

2. Put numbers on every job

  • Revenue achieved or target met: "120% of the annual target".
  • Portfolio: number of accounts managed, new clients won.
  • Growth: "+25% sales in the area in one year".
  • Average basket, conversion rate, retention, depending on your role.

No exact figures? Give an honest order of magnitude: "around sixty business clients".

3. Say what kind of selling

Retail, field sales, telesales, key accounts, B2B or B2C: these are different jobs. The recruiter should understand in one line what you did and who you sold to.

4. The tools and skills that matter

CRM software, Excel for sales tracking, negotiation, prospecting, a driving licence for field sales, the languages you speak with clients. Group them by category.

5. A layout that makes an impression

A sales CV can have some colour: it shows you know how to present yourself. Still keep it to one page, two at most with long experience, and put your strongest results at the top.
