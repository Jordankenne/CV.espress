Make a location map, for free

In many cities, streets have no name or number. For an administrative file, a bank, an employer or a delivery, you are then asked for a location map: a simple sketch showing how to reach your home from a place everyone knows.

When do you need a location map?

  • Grant, competitive exam or school enrolment files.
  • Opening a bank account or applying for a loan.
  • Job applications, water or electricity connection requests.
  • Registering a business or a shop.
  • So that a visitor, a delivery driver or a taxi can find your address.

What a good map contains

A starting point everyone knows: a main junction, a roundabout, a petrol station, a church, a mosque, a market, a school. The route starts from there.

The roads to follow, with the turns in the right direction, and arrows showing the way.

Landmarks along the way: an easy-to-recognise building at each change of direction.

Your home clearly marked, with a short description: "blue gate, second house on the left after the shop".

Your name and phone number, so people can call you if in doubt.

The GPS position, if possible. With CV.express, a QR code printed on the map opens the map straight away on the phone of whoever scans it.

How to make your map with CV.express

  1. Open the "Location map" tab. Answer the questions of the route assistant (starting point, turns, landmarks): it draws a first map for you.
  2. Adjust it with the mouse or your finger: move a road, add a building, write a name.
  3. When you are at home, tap "Use my current location" to add the GPS coordinates.
  4. Download the PDF and print it. It fits on one page, landscape or portrait. The title is yours to change.

Tips

Keep the map simple: a clear sketch is better than a very detailed map. Write names the way people in the neighbourhood say them. Have someone who does not know the way test your map before you send it.
