Make a sworn statement (« attestation sur l'honneur »)

A sworn statement is a written declaration in which you state that a fact is true: that you receive no grant, that you live at a given address, that you are single… It is often requested in a file when you cannot provide any other proof. The document is written in French, with the wording used by French-speaking administrations.

What the statement must contain

  • The title « Attestation sur l'honneur ».
  • Your full identity: name, date and place of birth, nationality, ID number, address.
  • The declaration itself, precise and unambiguous.
  • A sentence saying you know the penalties for a false declaration.
  • The closing formula, place, date and your handwritten signature.

Be careful

A false sworn statement is punishable by law. Only declare what is true. Depending on the administration, the signature sometimes has to be certified (town hall, police station or consulate): check before you submit your file.

Other documents of the same kind

Power of attorney (« procuration »): you authorise someone else to carry out a formality on your behalf. Both people sign.

Sponsorship statement (« attestation de prise en charge »): you commit to covering someone's costs during a stay (studies, visit, medical care), often for a visa application. Attach proof of your income.
