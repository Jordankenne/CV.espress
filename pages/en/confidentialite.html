Privacy policy

In short: without an account, what you write stays in your browser, and our servers only receive your information for the time needed to build your document. If you choose to create an account, your CV is also stored on our servers so that you can find it anywhere, until you delete it. No advertising cookies, no reselling of data.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is {{ champ("nom", "nom de l'éditeur ou raison sociale") }}, {{ champ("adresse", "adresse postale") }}. For any question about your data: {{ champ("email", "adresse e-mail") }}. Full details of the publisher and the hosting provider are in the legal notice.

The information you enter, without an account

Your name, contact details, personal details (date and place of birth, nationality, family situation), your background, your photo and the text of your letter are saved on your device, in your browser's local storage. This lets you find your CV again when you come back to the site on the same device. We have no access to it.

When the preview updates or when you download your PDF, this information is sent to our server over an encrypted connection. The server builds the document in a temporary folder, sends it back to you, then deletes that folder immediately. We do not store your information in any database, we do not read it, we do not sell it and we do not pass it on to anyone.

The editor's “Save” button creates a file on your device: it does not go through our servers.

If you import a CV or letter you already have (a PDF or Word file), that file is sent to our server, which reads its text to fill in the form, then deletes it immediately. It is neither kept nor passed on.

If you create an account

The account is optional: the site works entirely without one. If you create one, we store on our servers:

  • your e-mail address, which is your login;
  • your password as an encrypted fingerprint: we do not know your password and cannot recover it;
  • the content of your CV and your letter, photo included, so that you can find them on another device;
  • if you use the application tracker: the companies, positions, dates, statuses, links and notes you enter in it;
  • the date the account was created, of your last login and of the last change.

Online CV. If you turn on the sharing link of a document (“My space”), its CV can be viewed at a public address (/cv/…) by anyone who has that link or scans the QR code printed on your CV, including your name, contact details, photo and background. The address contains a random code that cannot be guessed, and the page is excluded from search engines. We only count the number of visits, without knowing who makes them. You can turn the link off at any time: the page stops existing at once.

Contact page. The message you send from the Contact page (with your name and e-mail address if you give them) is passed on to us by e-mail through our provider Brevo, so that we can reply. It is not stored on the site. To follow how the site is used, we also count, day by day, the number of pages viewed and documents created, with nothing that reveals who did it (no account, no IP address, no cookie).

This information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete it at any time from the “My space” page (“Delete my account”): the account and the stored CV are then erased from our servers immediately and permanently. We do not sell your documents or pass them on to anyone. The site administrator may view and correct an account and its documents, only to help you at your request, to fix an error or to stop abuse; each access is recorded in the server log. We do not send you any advertising e-mail: you only receive the messages you ask for: the link to confirm your address and choose your password when you create your account, and the link to choose a new one if you have forgotten it. An account is only created once that link is opened, which ensures nobody can create an account with your address. It is sent through our e-mail delivery provider, which receives your address for that purpose.

Each account only has access to its own data. When you log out, your CV is erased from the device you used: remember to log out on a shared computer.

Why we process it, and on what basis

  • Building your CV and your letter: this is the service you ask for. Legal basis: performance of the service you are using.
  • Managing your account and keeping your CV, if you create one: same legal basis, you ask for it by signing up.
  • Protecting the site against abuse and keeping it secure (technical logs, limit on the number of documents per minute): legal basis, our legitimate interest in running a reliable service.

We do no targeted advertising, no profiling and no automated decision-making about you.

Sensitive information

A CV only needs information that is useful for recruitment. You are never required to fill in personal details or to add a photo. Avoid including information about your health, religion, political opinions or trade-union membership: it is not necessary and, in most countries, an employer is not allowed to take it into account.

If your CV mentions other people (for example referees with their phone number), make sure they agree.

How to erase your information

In the editor, the “Clear everything” button deletes everything saved on your device. You can also clear the site's data from your browser settings. On a shared computer (internet café, school), remember to do so before leaving, and to delete the PDF from the “Downloads” folder.

Cookies and audience measurement

The site uses no advertising cookie, no tracking tool, no third-party analytics service, and no font or script loaded from another site. It only uses your browser's local storage described above and, if you log in to an account, a session cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 days at most. Both are essential to the service you ask for, so they do not require your consent. The cookie is deleted when you log out.

Technical logs

Like any website, our hosting provider records, for each visit, the IP address, the date, the page requested and the type of browser. These logs are used to keep the service secure and to limit abuse. Retention period: {{ champ("duree_journaux", "durée, par exemple « 12 mois »") }}.

To limit abuse, the server also keeps your IP address in memory for about one minute, in order to count the number of documents requested. It is not written to disk.

These logs do not contain the content of your CV. If building a document fails, we only record the type of technical error, without the text you entered, so that we can fix the problem.

We may be required to hand over technical logs to a judicial authority that lawfully requests them.

Sharing

If you use a share button (WhatsApp for example), the app in question opens, with a message containing only the site's address. Your CV is not shared. That app's own privacy rules apply.

Security

Exchanges with the site are encrypted (HTTPS). Each document is built in an isolated folder, deleted straight afterwards. Passwords are never stored in clear text, and repeated login attempts are blocked. The site loads no external script. As no system is perfectly secure, we advise you not to put on your CV information you do not need (ID document number, full address…).

Minors

The service may be used by young people looking for an internship or a first job. Without an account, no data is stored on our side. Creating an account is reserved for people old enough to consent on their own to the processing of their data in their country (15 in France, for example); below that age, ask a parent for permission. If you are a minor, it is best to ask a parent or a teacher to help you decide what information to put on your CV.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its use, to object to its processing and to obtain a copy of it. If you have an account, you see and edit your data directly in the editor, and the “My space” page lets you download a copy, change your password and delete everything. Without an account, we only hold the technical logs described above, linked to an IP address: so that we can find them, tell us your IP address and the date of your visit.

Write to {{ champ("email", "adresse e-mail") }}. We reply within one month.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country: the CNIL in France, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali in Italy, the CDP in Senegal, the ARTCI in Côte d'Ivoire, or the equivalent authority elsewhere.
