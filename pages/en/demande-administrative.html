Write a formal request to an authority, in French

A request addressed to a director, a minister, a mayor or a head of department follows a precise layout and expected polite phrases. CV.express drafts the text for you from your answers: you only need to read it over. The letter is written in French, in the style used by administrations in French-speaking Africa.

Available requests

  • Job or internship request, to attach to your CV.
  • Grant or enrolment request.
  • Permit, transfer or land allocation request.
  • Any other request, with a subject of your choice.

To contest a decision or report a problem, choose "Complaint or appeal" in the same tab instead.

The expected layout

Top left: your name, address and phone number. On the right: place and date, then the authority you are writing to. Then the subject in one line, the salutation repeating the person's title, a body in three parts (what you ask for, why, closing formula), your signature and the list of enclosures.

Handwritten?

Some administrations require a handwritten request. In that case, prepare the text here, print it, then copy it by hand onto a sheet of paper with the same layout. Add a revenue stamp if one is required, and sign.

How to do it with CV.express

  1. Open the "Administrative documents" tab and choose "Request to an authority".
  2. Choose the type of request and the authority, and explain your situation in a few sentences.
  3. The text writes itself. Passages in [brackets] are left for you to complete. You can then edit it freely.
  4. Download the PDF.
