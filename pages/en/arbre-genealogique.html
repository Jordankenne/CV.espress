Create a family tree, for free

A family tree shows a family over several generations: an ancestor, their children, their grandchildren. People make one to keep the family's memory alive, for a reunion or a funeral, or because a file requires it, for an inheritance for example.

Starting from an ancestor

On CV.express, the tree grows downwards: you enter an ancestor (a grandfather, a great-grandmother), then their unions, then the children of each union, and so on. It is the clearest way to show all the descendants of one person.

Several wives or husbands

In many families, an ancestor had several unions, each with its own children. The tree shows this directly: each spouse is linked to their children. A child whose other parent is unknown can be added too.

What to include

  • Each person's full name, as used in the family.
  • Years of birth and death, if you know them: they help place each generation.
  • The place of birth, when useful (home village, for example).

Start with what you know, then ask the elders of the family to fill the gaps. You can save your work and come back to it later.

How to make your tree with CV.express

  1. Open the "Family tree" tab and write the ancestor's name.
  2. Add their unions and children, then the children of each child.
  3. Choose whether to show dates and places, and set a title ("The Mbarga family").
  4. Download the PDF. A large family automatically moves to a bigger page (A3) so it stays readable.
